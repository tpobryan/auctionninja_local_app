Label Processor

Convert Vinted A4 PDF labels into 4x6 thermal printer format.

How to use on iPhone:

  1. Open the Vinted app and download the shipping label.
  2. Tap the Share icon and choose Save to Files.
  3. Come to this page and upload that file.
  4. You will instantly receive a 4x6 PDF ready to print on your Rollo!
